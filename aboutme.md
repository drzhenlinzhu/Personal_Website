### 👋 Nice to meet you!

Hello, I'm Zhenlin (Cassie) Zhu, born and raised in Sichuan, China. When my dad brought home our family's first telescope, I fell in love with the night sky, and I've been fascinated by it ever since.

Driven by this passion, I pursued my bachelor’s and master’s degrees in the Department of Astronomy and Space Science at Nanjing University. I later moved to the Netherlands to continue my academic journey at Leiden University, where I earned my PhD. During this time, I worked at the Netherlands Institute for Space Research (SRON) for five years, focusing on the X-ray emission of galaxy clusters. After my PhD, I moved to Los Angeles and worked at UCLA as an AI-for-Science data scientist.

### 🚀 From Astronomy to Data Science & Machine Learning

Throughout my research career, I have worked extensively with large-scale astronomical datasets, advanced statistical modeling, and coding skills that seamlessly translate into data science, machine learning, and quantitative research. From analyzing terabytes of observational data to building predictive models, I have developed strong expertise in Python, R, SQL, statistical modeling, A/B testing, and data storytelling. At UCLA, I independently designed research methodologies, worked with heterogeneous and unstructured data, and built analytical frameworks to tackle complex scientific questions.

In recent years, my interests have increasingly shifted toward AI and machine learning. Motivated by this transition, I worked as a Machine Learning Engineer intern on recommendation and retrieval models, and then as an AI Engineer training a domain-specific LLM and building AI agents for network troubleshooting. Along the way I built [hands-on projects]({{ '/projects' | relative_url }}) ranging from traditional classification tasks to Generative AI systems. I believe the rigorous thinking, problem-solving mindset, and quantitative training developed in academia make me well suited for data-driven roles in technology and finance.

### 🌍 Life beyond Research

Having grown up in Asia, completed my PhD in Europe, and now living in the United States, I’ve been fortunate to experience life across cultures. This journey has shaped me into a truly global citizen who enjoys working in multicultural environments and connecting with people from all over the world.

Outside of work, I love staying active—swimming, hiking, volleyball, and pickleball are my favorites. I also have a soft spot for fluffy companions, especially my 5-year-old ragdoll cat, Cocoa. Science is endlessly fascinating, but I believe life beyond research is just as important.

Data Scientist · AI Engineer · PhD Astrophysics

Hi, I'm Zhenlin (Cassie) Zhu.

I build machine-learning models and AI systems on large, messy data. For years that data came from X-ray telescopes; today I also train domain LLMs, build AI agents and ship recommendation models.

Most recently I was an AI engineer at Donglian Tech, and before that an AI-for-Science data scientist at UCLA. I hold a PhD in astrophysics from Leiden University and the Netherlands Institute for Space Research (SRON), and I'm looking for data science and AI roles in tech and finance.
